Capri & Ischia

₹ 3,99,000 / person
Bay of Naples, Italy

Rising sheer from the azure Mediterranean waters, Capri and Ischia embody the height of Italian coastal glamor and thermal wellness.
From limestone sea stacks and hidden luminescent sea caves to volcanic mineral thermal spas, they offer timeless Mediterranean elegance.

Adventure

  • Private wooden gozzo boat cruises around the iconic Faraglioni rock monoliths and anchoring beneath the White and Green Grottoes
  • Entering the famous Blue Grotto (Grotta Azzurra) on small rowing rowboats to witness its electric cobalt-blue luminescent water
  • Relaxing in the ancient volcanic thermal mineral springs and volcanic mud baths of Poseidon Gardens on Ischia
  • Walking along the Via Krupp cliffside pathway and trekking the panoramic trails of Mount Solaro via open-air single-chairlift
  • Exploring the 2,500-year-old Aragonese Castle (Castello Aragonese) perched dramatically on a volcanic islet connected by causeway

Cuisine

  • Insalata Caprese: sliced sweet sun-ripened Sorrento oxheart tomatoes, creamy buffalo mozzarella from Campania, fresh basil, and extra virgin olive oil
  • Ravioli Capresi: delicate handmade pasta pockets filled with aged caciotta cheese, fresh marjoram, and tossed in light cherry tomato sauce
  • Coniglio all'Ischitana: Ischia's traditional braised rabbit stew cooked in clay earthenware with local white wine, garlic, cherry tomatoes, and wild thyme
  • Torta Caprese: flourless dark chocolate and almond cake dusted with powdered sugar, originating from a chef's serendipitous baking mistake in the 1920s
  • Chilled artisanal Limoncello di Capri crafted from the fragrant, thick rinds of hand-picked Sorrento and Capri oval lemons

Facts

  • Roman Emperors Augustus and Tiberius ruled the entire Roman Empire from Capri; Tiberius built twelve imperial villas here, including Villa Jovis
  • Ischia is one of the oldest volcanic islands in the Mediterranean, possessing over a hundred natural therapeutic geothermal hot mineral springs
  • The luminescent glow of the Blue Grotto is created by sunlight passing through a deep underwater opening directly beneath the cave entrance
  • Re-emerged in the 1950s and 60s during the Dolce Vita era as the international retreat for Hollywood stars, writers, and European royalty
  • Capri’s Piazzetta, originally the local produce marketplace, is affectionately nicknamed 'The World’s Living Room'
  • Cultivates volcanic vineyards producing crisp, mineral-forward DOC wines like Biancolella and Forastera grown on vertical terrace walls
