Privacy Policy

Varidhi Incorporation Ltd. — Information Governance, Client-Side Architecture & Personal Protection Standards.

— Privacy Policy and Data Protection

The Varidhi web portal operates on a pure static, client-side frontend architecture engineered with standard HTML5, CSS3, and ECMAScript. There is absolutely no backend web server, database management system, cloud data warehouse, or intermediary API connected to this platform. Every interactive computation — including calculating custom group package quotes, viewing advance deposit splits, and navigating through our worldwide island catalog — is executed entirely within your personal device's local web browser engine.

Because this website operates without a backend infrastructure, user inputs, group sizes, browsing behaviors, and device identifiers are never transmitted across the network to any Varidhi server or centralized database. It is technically impossible for our platform to harvest, intercept, track, or remotely store your private online interactions. This design inherently respects global data protection benchmarks, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

— Data Control and User Information

Our digital philosophy prioritizes data minimization: the most protected data is information that is never gathered. When you interact with our reservation calculator or browse package itineraries, all state changes occur in volatile browser RAM and are purged instantly upon refreshing the page or closing the browser window. Our platform does not employ persistent tracking cookies, session-recording telemetry, keystroke loggers, or background scrapers.

Varidhi maintains zero relationships with commercial ad brokers, retargeting agencies, or data resellers. We do not deploy Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook) Tracking Pixels, or third-party behavioral beacons. Any static assets, such as high-resolution photographs and typography stylesheets, are cached solely within your browser's local memory under your direct sovereign control, and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

— Personal Safety

When travelers decide to proceed with an expedition reservation and communicate directly with Varidhi Incorporation Ltd. through verified corporate email, telephone, or encrypted messaging, all submitted information is managed under strict executive discretion. Sensitive traveler details — including passport identification, emergency contacts, medical declarations, and flight arrival schedules — are collected voluntarily and solely to fulfill legal booking contracts, arrange private aviation clearances, and secure private island villa compounds.

All digital communications exchanged with our concierge team are protected by standard transport layer security (TLS 1.3) and enterprise-grade encryption. Access to traveler manifests is strictly limited to authorized founding directors and vetted logistics leads who require the information to coordinate port arrivals and customs protocols with accredited island suppliers.

— Liability and Maritime Safety Guidelines

Our signature 15-day island journeys operate across fragile marine ecosystems, including designated UNESCO Biosphere reserves. For the collective safety of all guests and the preservation of protected marine habitats, all travelers must rigorously adhere to maritime safety protocols and obey the operational directives issued by certified yacht captains, seaplane commanders, and marine park rangers.

Varidhi maintains a strict environmental code prohibiting contact with live coral reefs, harassment of marine species, or violation of sovereign maritime sanctuaries. Furthermore, while Varidhi manages all scheduled transfers, private charters, and guided excursions from Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi onwards, travelers who engage in independent personal sports, unguided ocean swims, or private activities outside the structured itinerary and guidelines provided by Varidhi do so at their own risk, with Varidhi assuming no liability or financial responsibility for such independent pursuits.

— Force Majeure and Unforeseen Circumstances

Expeditions across remote archipelagos are inherently subject to natural marine phenomena. In the event of force majeure occurrences — including tropical cyclones, tsunamis, seismic or volcanic activity, sudden ocean squalls, closed airspaces, or sovereign border restrictions — passenger safety takes absolute precedence over published schedules.

If local maritime advisories render a scheduled inter-island catamaran crossing or reef excursion unsafe, Varidhi dynamically modifies the daily itinerary, substituting luxury land safaris, sheltered bay cruises, or private estate cultural experiences of equal value. If severe weather or official government travel closures prevent an entire expedition from commencing prior to departure, Varidhi issues a 100% Lifetime Future Travel Credit Voucher valid across all 10 island sanctuaries with zero expiration penalty.
