OUR STORY & PHILOSOPHY

✨ Crafted for Conversation, Engineered for Zero Waiting

Born in 2022, ChromaCafé started with a simple question: why should great café hospitality be interrupted by waving for menus or waiting 20 minutes for the bill?

We combined specialty 100% Arabica coffee roasters, a 400°C Neapolitan pizza oven, and an interactive table-ordering ecosystem so our baristas and chefs can focus 100% on craft and flavour.

🌱 Farm-to-Cup

Direct-trade Chikmagalur coffee beans & organic greenhouse produce.

⚡ Table-First Tech

Live kitchen telemetry and collaborative group ordering at every table.

🎨 Artisanal Craft

Zero pre-made bases. Every sauce, dough, and ganache is made fresh daily.
